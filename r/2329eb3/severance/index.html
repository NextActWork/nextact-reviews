Read what your severance text actually says

Paste any part of it and this page will read back the money, the dates, the health-coverage sentences and the obligations it can find in that text — without sending what you paste anywhere.

What you paste stays in this browser. No upload, no analytics, no server to send it to. What about the site itself?

The page's own code makes no network requests, which is tested by serving it and watching what leaves. The web host (Cloudflare) does add its own bot-detection script to pages it serves, as it does across the site; that script never sees the text in the box, but it means the served page is not literally request-free, and we would rather say so than claim otherwise.

If you want to talk to a lawyer

Not a recommendation, and not a referral. NextAct selects no lawyer, takes no payment from any of them, and no listing here is an endorsement — each one is shown with a sentence from its own page so you can judge it yourself.

Used for one thing only: to add directories for that state below. It changes no arithmetic on this page, and it does not decide which state's law applies to you. The national directory shows either way.

How the counting works. We begin counting with the day after the starting date you give us. If your agreement states a different convention, follow your agreement — this tool cannot override it.

Business days. We skip weekends but treat public holidays as ordinary weekdays. Your employer's holiday calendar may therefore produce a later date than we show. Confirm any business-day result against a calendar.

What this does not do. It does not tell you whether your agreement is fair, enforceable, or compliant with any law, what claims it releases, whether to sign it, or what concessions or negotiation positions to request. It performs arithmetic on dates you supply.
