How long is the job search, really?

Time to reemployment in the US isn't one number — it depends heavily on where you are in your career. Here's what the latest labor-market data shows, by stage.

Typical search (median)

11.6 weeks

how long the typical unemployed worker is looking

Average search (mean)

26.0 weeks

Long-term unemployed

27.5%

Sources: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics — Current Population Survey. Overall figures are May 2026, seasonally adjusted (median & mean duration, Table A-12; long-term share, 27+ weeks). By-career-stage figures are average (mean) duration, 2025 annual averages (Table A-31). BLS "duration" measures time for people currently still unemployed, so it understates completed time-to-reemployment.
