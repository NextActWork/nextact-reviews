Privacy and limits, in full

What this does not do. NextAct does not decide whether a law was broken, does not tell you which channel is right for you, does not rank these channels or order them by any award, does not file anything, does not receive your report, and does not create a lawyer–client relationship. Each channel below is shown with a sentence from its own official page so that you can judge whether it fits — that judgment is yours, and a lawyer can help you make it.

What we count. We record the name of a step you reach — for example that the questions were started, that a result was shown, or that a channel link was opened. That name is the only page data we send, and it never includes anything you selected: not what you saw, not who was harmed, not the setting, not your employer type, not any date. Each event is kept as a separate anonymous record for up to 180 days and used only to produce totals; the records are not linked to an account, to you, or to one another. The request carries no cookie and no referrer.
