Why we track this

Every number is a person.

Behind every data point is someone who just lost a job and has to file an unemployment claim — a confusing, error-prone process that works differently in every state. That's why we built NextAct.work: it prepares the correct state unemployment-claim forms, filled in and ready to review and submit. It's our first product — the beginning of what NextActs is for.
