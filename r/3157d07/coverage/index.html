What this does not do. NextAct does not decide eligibility, predict a subsidy, rank plans, or tell you which coverage to choose. It applies published timing rules to facts you confirm and links you to official enrollment sites and certified help. Plan prices and eligibility are determined on those sites.

What we count. We record the name of a step you reach—for example that a state was chosen, that a result was shown, or simply whether the text you pasted contained anything we recognised. That name is the only page data we send. Each event is kept as a separate anonymous record for up to 180 days and used only to produce totals; the records are not linked to an account, to you, or to one another. We never send which state you chose, which field a detail came from, your dates, any premium you type, the text you paste, or any value shown to you—those stay in your browser. The request carries no cookie and no referrer.
